RUHS Medical Officer Direct Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 600 Post

🏛Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS), Jaipur

💼Latest JobsOfficial update guide

Rajasthan University of Health Sciences has invited online applications for 600 Medical Officer posts. Apply online from 14 September 2026 to 13 October 2026.

📅 Published 15 Sept 2026🕒 Updated 16 Sept 2026✍️ Written by: Sarkari Job Hub Editorial TeamEditorial Process & Verification📖 Read time: 3 min
Apply Online ↗

💳 Application Fee

Fee DetailsGeneral / OBC / EWS: ₹5000; Rajasthan State SC / ST: ₹2500. Pay through the prescribed online or offline fee mode.
Payment ModeDebit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI (as per official portal)

⏳ Age Limit

Age Limit Details22 to 45 years as on 01/01/2027. Age relaxation as per RUHS Medical Officer recruitment rules.

👥 Vacancy & Eligibility Details

Total Post600 Posts
Vacancy Information600 Medical Officer posts through RUHS Medical Officer Direct Recruitment 2026.
Educational QualificationMBBS degree from a recognized institution. Practical knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan culture. Check the official notification for complete eligibility.
Selection ProcessOnline Application → RUHS Medical Officer Examination → Document Verification → Final Selection

📖 About This Job Update

Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS), Jaipur has released the notification for RUHS Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 for 600 posts.

Eligible candidates can apply online from 14/09/2026 to 13/10/2026. The tentative examination date is 13/12/2026. Candidates should read the official notification for complete eligibility, syllabus, department-wise details, selection procedure and pay scale.
